# Pathé 259561 — Kaufman attribution

Updated: 2026-07-31

## Assessment

**Strongest identification: Dr. Bernard Kaufman Sr. (San Francisco) family travel film.**

A 24 May 1935 *Emanu-El and the Jewish Journal* notice says the Kaufmans’ destination was Palestine and their itinerary “a leisurely tour of Central Europe”—an almost exact match to Pathé’s later catalogue title. Subsequent notices explicitly advertise moving pictures taken on that trip and presented by Dr., Mrs., and Bernard Kaufman Jr.

Bernard Kaufman Sr. was an American cardiologist and Zionist, not a rabbi. Pathé’s “Rabbi R. Kaufman” and 1936 date are therefore probable later cataloguing errors (possibly corrupted from “Dr. B. Kaufman”). Confidence is high but not absolute until Pathé’s accession / donor record is located.

No original footage was opened for this attribution pass.

## Named places in contemporary Kaufman sources

**Trip notices (May 1935–Feb 1936):** only “Central Europe” and “Palestine” / “Jewish Homeland” / “Near East.” No Warsaw, Kutno, Otwock, Berlin, etc.

**Dr. Kaufman’s own post-trip articles (*Emanu-El*, Nov 1935–Jan 1936):**
- Palestine: Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Haifa, Petah Tikvah, Rishon LeZion, Rehovot (“Rechovah”)
- 8 Nov 1935: “Dr. Kaufman, Home from Palestine Discusses Immigration Problems” (to be concluded)
- 15 Nov 1935: concluding installment, “Palestine Faces New Era In Immigration”
- 17 Jan 1936: Legislative Council piece; refers to explanations “offered in Jerusalem”
- No named Central/Eastern European towns in these articles either

**Separate (not the 1935 itinerary):** family lived in Vienna in the 1920s (returned SF 1928); Sr. described as a European representative of the AMA.
