Filmkontor – the concept

Filmkontor is one of the largest archives of historical private films in Germany.

These unique scenes were created between 1918 and 1995. A treasure trove of cinematic tradition that makes every documentary come alive. We focus on quick service and comprehensive advice. Regardless of whether you are looking for a very specific motif or need global images to illustrate a city, an attitude towards life or an epoch.
