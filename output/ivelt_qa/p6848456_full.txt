0:51–1:22 — Removal of part of the cemetery

Czech

Ve středu města — a přece vzdálen jeho hluku, stojí starý židovský hřbitov. V 17. století se přestává zde pochovávat a klid mrtvých není rušen až v roce 1903, kdy rozhodnutím pražské obce musí být hranice hřbitova posunuta o celou šíři dnešní ulice Sanytrové, kde stojí nyní české akademické gymnasium. Dva tisíce náhrobních kamenů odklizeno a několik tisíc mrtvých pohřbeno znovu na návrší Nefele, kdež byl v roce 1906 pohřebním bratrstvem postaven pomník.

English

In the center of the city — and yet far from its noise — stands the old Jewish cemetery. In the 17th century burial here ceased, and the peace of the dead was undisturbed until 1903, when by decision of the Prague municipality the cemetery boundary had to be moved by the full width of today’s Sanytrová Street, where the Czech Academic Gymnasium now stands. Two thousand tombstones were cleared away, and several thousand of the dead were reburied on Nefele Hill, where in 1906 the burial society erected a monument.

1:53–2:21 — The tomb of Mordecai Meisel

Czech

— hrobky Mordechaje Meisela — syna Samuelova — který zemřel v roce 106 (hebr.). Tento velký dobrodinec vystavěl svým nákladem tak zvanou Vysokou synagogu i synagogu, jež má po něm jméno, dal zříditi lázně pro ženy, chudobinec, nemocnici a na svůj náklad dal také vydlážditi celé židovské město. Císařem byl mu propůjčen titul vládního rady.

English

— the tomb of Mordecai Meisel — son of Samuel — who died in the year 106 (Hebrew). This great benefactor built at his own expense the so-called High Synagogue and the synagogue that bears his name; he had baths for women, an almshouse, and a hospital established, and at his own expense also had the entire Jewish quarter paved. The Emperor conferred on him the title of Imperial Councillor.

English

— and as we leave we are directed to a tombstone on which a monk is carved in the center of the slab, with two roosters around him. Legend says that a certain Jew, having had himself baptized, entered a monastery. His last wish before death was to be buried in the Jewish cemetery.
