    Short Summary

    Material relating to newsreel story - "The Papal Pilgrimage" - 64/6.

    Description

    Selected Originals (offcuts, out-takes, selected scenes, rushes) for newsreel story "The Papal Pilgrimage" - 64/6. Various Technicolor and Techniscope (widescreen) cuts for visit of Pope Paul VI to the Holy Land. Various GVs of Holy Sites and of frontiers. Pope at Sea of Galilee. Pope visiting various sites. Shots of Palestinian towns. Young boy goat herd with flock. Fishermen at Galilee. Various Churches. GVs of towns. CUs of various paintings from different collections. Shots of places in Israel. Orthodox Jewish boys and men. Shots of places in Jordan. Shots of Bethlehem. Various CUs of painting - some not used in finished item. Various sets of titles. Followed by double speed mute version of entire colour story. Cataloguer's Note: This material has been transferred at the wrong speed - MD.
